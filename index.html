Privacy Policy

Tap Tap Blue Bird

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Thank you for playing Tap Tap Blue Bird ("the Game"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive and safe experience playing our game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play the Game on iOS devices.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address to play the Game.

A. Automatically Collected Information

Depending on your device settings and enabled features, certain non-personal or pseudonymous technical data may be processed locally or by platform services:

  • Gameplay Data: High scores, game progress, and in-game achievements are stored locally on your device (and synced via Apple Game Center if enabled).
  • Device & Performance Data: Anonymous diagnostic, performance, and crash logs to help identify bugs and improve stability.

B. Apple Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Apple Game Center, Apple handles your Game Center profile, leaderboards, and achievements in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not store your Game Center credentials on external servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

If the Game incorporates third-party SDKs (such as Apple Frameworks, analytics, or ad networks), those third parties may collect anonymous identifiers subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework and their respective privacy policies:

  • Apple Services: App Store in-app purchases, Game Center, and Crashlytics/Diagnostics.
  • Tracking Permission: We will never track you across apps and websites owned by other companies without your explicit permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Age Compliance)

We respect the privacy of children. The Game does not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age limit in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such data.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not maintain central user accounts or store personal data on proprietary external servers, your gameplay data is tied to your local device installation and Apple iCloud/Game Center backup. You can delete local game data at any time by uninstalling the Game from your iOS device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.
